= 10. Quality Requirements
:toc:
:sectnums:

Status: implementation-informed. Seven bounded Maven move-class definitions expanding to twelve cases are implemented and validated: CLI lexical-fallback refusal without residue (`005`), the complete `AVAILABLE` reverse-observer-closure warning boundary (`006`), one synthetic closure-missing `OFFLINE_MISSING` apply/rollback row (`007`), explicit transitive-test-scope preservation with an `AVAILABLE` target closure while global `OFFLINE_MISSING` remains exclusively outside it (`009`), descriptor-closed selector pruning plus alternate selected-path missing-POM refusal (`010`), RefactorKit-owned sidecar-free ordinary selected-JAR absence with three under-lock pre-WAL drift refusals (`011`), and six selected-descriptor `MISSING`/`MALFORMED`/`DRIFTED` refusals with stable context/hashes and no binary-absence downgrade or residue (`012`). REQ-JAVA-MAVEN-MOVE-AUTH-008 remains executable and absent. Full/general positive apply, cross-surface coverage, and native-matrix qualification remain open. The exact Magrathea request at commit `a48e3c06` is separately CLI-qualified on Linux/JDK 21 after `010`; this field result did not require `011` or `012`. Other priorities and measurable targets need review.

** Preview before apply
** Reject stale or unsafe edits

** Warnings for high-risk Java/framework/import cases
* Correctness
** Symbol-aware Java operations where supported
** Conservative refusal instead of guessing
** Diagnostics before/after preview where supported
* Java/Maven diagnostics authority
** RefactorKit semantic evidence is definitive; Maven builds are supplementary
** Exact release-aware Java platforms and full-reactor source-set environments
** Typed unavailability and bounded cascade suppression instead of fabricated errors
** Default no-introduced-errors and optional strict-zero staged policy
* P0 Maven move-class authority
** Every active reactor POM remains required; each main/test projection is descriptor-closed over selected external nodes, and every outgoing declaration has a hash-bound selected or exact scope/optional/exclusion-pruned result

** Outside-closure `OFFLINE_MISSING` remains visible without provided-type or per-binary operation inventory only after complete graph and candidate exclusion are proved
** Candidate-total `BOUND_TARGET` / `BOUND_OTHER` / `UNRESOLVED` JDT classification; target-relevant uncertainty blocks and only exact target bindings select edits
** Implemented bounded RefactorKit-owned ordinary evidence covers one fixed-release simple local Maven-2-layout selected `jar` graph leaf with complete present POM closure and an absent binary, sidecar-free no-follow absence, no invented absent-content hash or provided-type inventory, exact source binding, and target-name lookup protection
** Exact unchanged mapped identity and positive non-concealment proof for every retained closure diagnostic
** No new or changed staged error and no structural, artifact, candidate, provider, or diagnostic evidence drift
** Review-only lexical evidence and pre-WAL refusal on any authority loss; lexical inventory is veto-only
** Explicit non-managed guided fallback with no applyable plan
** Permanent 20-active-module fixture created; bounded closure-missing `007`, explicit-scope/outside-closure `009`, descriptor-pruning/alternate-selected-POM `010`, ordinary selected-JAR `011`, and six-case selected-descriptor refusal `012` accepted, while executable `008`, full/general positive apply, cross-surface, and native-matrix qualification remain open; the exact Magrathea CLI field request is separately qualified after `010`
